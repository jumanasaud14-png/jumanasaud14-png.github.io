Voice revision — 28 September 2026 (Riyadh)
The separate namour-ar-voice.mp3 and namour-en-voice.mp3 files were generated through Runway text-to-speech with the Rachel preset and Eleven Multilingual v2, using explicit Arabic/English language codes. Arabic was supplied with pronunciation marks. No cloned voice or impersonation was used. Sentence pauses longer than 0.45 seconds were shortened to 0.38 seconds; spoken audio was not sped up or pitch shifted. The final videos use these recordings directly, replacing the video model's generated dialogue.

To the extent the pilot contributors hold rights in the standalone voice recordings, those rights are dedicated under CC0 1.0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ .
